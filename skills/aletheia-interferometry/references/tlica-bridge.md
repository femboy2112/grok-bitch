## 1. Coordinate separation

### κ — contact

Ask how directly the evidence channel is causally coupled to the object. High contact can improve
measurement relevance; it does not establish truth. Low contact makes a dim field more likely and
should limit evidential weight.

### φ — truth-indistinguishability

Ask how well the claim survives the verification pathway constructible from the current toolkit.
φ is not felt confidence. For ordinary contents it is toolkit- and decomposition-relative. When no
verification pathway is constructible, φ is **undefined**, not zero and not false.

Operational rule: an undefined φ-pathway is a pathway-gap. First generate a path, tool, comparator,
or source articulation. Do not manufacture a score for a content outside the current reason-test
economy.

### ρ — identity-correlation and commitment risk

Ask how structurally bound the reasoner, project, institution, or self-model is to the claim. ρ is
not evidence. High ρ predicts a larger risk of defensive assimilation, premature collapse,
post-hoc rescue, or overreaction to criticism. Increase blinding, hostile review, mutation tests,
and preregistration as ρ-risk rises.

### σ — source map

Ask what object, process, record, implementation, or causal route produced the content. A content
can remain truth-indistinguishable under available probes while being systematically mis-sourced.
This is why the interferometer supports a product basis `H_content x H_source` and provenance-aware
probes.

### μ — probe measure

Ask which probes receive actual attention, resources, and positive operational probability. A
perfect discriminator at measure zero is practically absent. Promote decisive probes into the
active schedule, especially those that open a new contact or source route.

### Cl(Tools) — tool closure

Search not only currently deployed tools but lawful compositions, decompositions, reformulations,
basis changes, independent implementations, and generated probes. An apparent cave may be
metastable when a discriminator already lies in tool closure but has not been surfaced.

## 2. Truth light and object configuration

The metaphor becomes precise as follows:

- **Light** corresponds to the constraints imposed by reality on possible observations.
- **Object configuration** corresponds to the lawful forward maps through which the latent object is
  contacted.
- **The observed pattern** is the configuration-dependent measurement, including bias and noise.
- **Back-transport** removes known configuration effects.
- **Constructive interference** is lawful cross-configuration agreement after provenance adjustment.
- **Destructive interference** is structured cancellation that localizes a missing distinction.

The object is not inferred from brightness alone. It is inferred from the transformation law of the
whole pattern across configurations.

## 3. Contextual saturation, not finality

When surviving hypotheses are indistinguishable under the current tool closure and there is no
known source-map or probe-coverage defect, the result is **contextually saturated**. It is legitimate
to act on as the current dominant model, but it is not an exterior certificate that no larger tool
closure could split the equivalence class.

Record:

```text
current equivalence class
available tool closure
source-map audit
probe-measure audit
known residuals
next possible path-opening operation
```

This preserves TLICA's cofinal-ascent discipline: a better frame dominates by explaining prior
successes and residuals, not by claiming to have exited every possible cave.

## 4. Cave diagnostics

- **Pathway-closure:** a rival or content has no constructible verification path. Generate a basis,
  source articulation, model, or tool composition.
- **Probe-closure:** the path exists but no discriminating probe has positive μ. Reallocate attention
  or change contact.
- **Source-closure:** all current tests observe the content but not the route that generated it.
  Expand into claim-source hypotheses.
- **Identity lock:** high ρ prevents cheap incremental revision. Separate critique of the claim from
  annihilation of the person or project, while preserving full falsifiability.

## 5. TLICA-aware output vector

Never reduce the result to a single confidence number. Report at least:

```text
κ/contact quality
φ pathway state and survival boundary
ρ commitment risk
σ/source-map adequacy
μ/probe-closure status
independence/dependence map
coherence and incoherent power
holdout phase lock
claim-ledger status
```

The vector makes dissociations visible. High coherence with weak σ is not the same epistemic state
as high coherence with an independently verified source map.
