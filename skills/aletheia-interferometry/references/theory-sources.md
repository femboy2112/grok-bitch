## Classical discrimination and interaction machinery

- The Bhattacharyya coefficient is used only as a bounded overlap measure between finite predictive
  distributions. It marks observational indistinguishability under a chosen probe, not ontological
  identity or quantum-state coherence.
- Complete factorial contrasts and the Walsh–Hadamard transform supply the exact classical algebra
  for separating means, main effects, and configuration interactions in binary designs. A nonzero
  interaction establishes non-additivity at the measured scale; causal interpretation still requires
  controls and rival mechanism predictions.
- Expected information gain is used as one experiment-design component, not as a truth score. It is
  combined with unresolved-pair separation, source-map sensitivity, provenance novelty, calibration,
  reliability, and cost.

## Quantum and coherent-processing analogies

These sources support the mathematical analogies and the safeguards; they do not establish that
truth-seeking or cognition is physically quantum.

- Emmanuel M. Pothos and Jerome R. Busemeyer, “A Quantum Probability Explanation for Violations of
  ‘Rational’ Decision Theory,” *Proceedings of the Royal Society B* 276 (2009), 2171–2178,
  doi:10.1098/rspb.2009.0121. Quantum probability is used as a formal probabilistic framework while
  distinguishing that use from a claim that the brain is a physical quantum computer.
- Gilles Brassard, Peter Høyer, Michele Mosca, and Alain Tapp, “Quantum Amplitude Amplification and
  Estimation,” arXiv:quant-ph/0005055. The quadratic query result depends on actual quantum access
  assumptions; the classical Aletheia search heuristic claims no such speedup.
- C. W. Helstrom, *Quantum Detection and Estimation Theory* (Academic Press, 1976). The state-
  discrimination analogy motivates selecting an observation that maximally separates surviving
  candidate response signatures; Aletheia uses a transparent classical contrast heuristic instead
  of claiming a physical quantum measurement.
- Coherent radar, synthetic-aperture, and interferometric imaging practice motivates phase correction
  before coherent integration: lawful configuration and propagation effects must be compensated or
  real structure defocuses. Aletheia applies the same discipline abstractly to signed/contextual
  evidence while preserving source provenance.

## Design nonclaims

1. Complex vectors are bookkeeping devices.
2. Visibility and amplitude are not truth probabilities.
3. Coherence can result from shared bias.
4. Cancellation can result from heterogeneity or a wrong transport.
5. Classical execution has classical complexity.
6. Proof, calibrated measurement, causal identification, and domain statistics retain priority.
