# Quantum Guardrails

Aletheia Interferometry is **quantum-inspired and classically executed by default**.

## Operational correspondences

- **superposition** → retain several materially distinct live hypotheses;
- **measurement basis** → choose a probe family or representation exposing a needed distinction;
- **overlap/coherence** → measure similarity of rival predictive distributions;
- **interference** → measure a lawful phasor cross-term or factorial interaction;
- **which-path information** → retain provenance and dependence rather than erasing it;
- **decoherence** → obtain genuinely independent evidence that destroys a current equivalence class;
- **amplitude allocation** → allocate search budget toward high-mass unresolved branches;
- **tomographic intuition** → choose a compact, preregistered probe portfolio that identifies the
  structural degrees of freedom relevant to the task.

These are classical definitions and design analogies. They do not imply that truth, language,
reasoning, consciousness, or the investigated object is a quantum state.

## Claims forbidden without an actual quantum implementation

Do not claim:

- semantic hypotheses held coherently on a quantum processor;
- Grover or amplitude-amplification quadratic speedup;
- amplitude-estimation or phase-estimation complexity;
- physical entanglement between evidence sources;
- Born-rule probabilities that propositions are true;
- classical-shadow guarantees for arbitrary non-quantum claims;
- quantum advantage, supremacy, or reduced asymptotic complexity.

An actual quantum claim requires all of:

1. a defined Hilbert-space encoding;
2. feasible state preparation;
3. a specified oracle or unitary and its query/gate cost;
4. a measurement protocol;
5. a noise and error model;
6. a classical baseline with the same access model;
7. end-to-end resource accounting, including loading and readout.

## Phase discipline

Use phase only when supplied before observation by a signed, oriented, lagged, parity, Fourier,
character, or genuinely complex-valued prediction plus a declared transport into a common basis.
Agreement among prose reports is not physical constructive interference. A fitted sign assigned after
seeing the result is not a prediction.

## Correct language

Use:

> “This is a classical quantum-inspired experiment-design protocol. It borrows the logic of basis
> choice, predictive overlap, which-path integrity, and cross-term measurement; it does not claim
> quantum computation or quantum speedup.”
