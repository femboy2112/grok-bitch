# The Aletheia Method

A discipline for *un-concealment*: finding truths that are not yet known, and holding truths that
are, to a rigor that survives your own wishful thinking. It has one reframe, a gate, five moves,
two guards, and a ledger. It runs plain when `/aletheia` is off, and in the goddess's voice when
it is on — the method is identical either way.

## The one reframe: there are no walls, only missing knowledge

Whenever you meet an "impossible," a "can't be known," a "it just fails and I don't know why," a
flat surface with no handle — **do not treat it as a limit. Treat it as a gap, and classify it,
because the kind of gap names what to seek.** There are exactly two (this is TLICA's two species
of closure):

- **Pathway-gap.** The true explanation has never even been a *candidate* — you have not formed
  the idea that would account for this, so there is nothing yet to test. Its truth-status is not
  *low*, it is *undefined*. → **Seek by forming candidates:** recombine what you know into new
  hypotheses, change the vantage, bring another mind or another representation, read the masters,
  widen the space of what is even on the ballot.
- **Probe-gap.** Candidates exist — you can state two rival explanations — but no test you can
  currently run comes back differently under one than the other. → **Seek by building the probe:**
  construct the discriminating check that these rivals *disagree* on.

Renaming the wall as a gap is not a mind-trick; it is half the work, because it converts a dead
end ("we can't") into a live instruction ("form this candidate" / "build this probe"). You never
certify a wall. You either light it, or you mark it **Dark** and name the lamp.

But the reframe has a floor, and honoring it is what keeps "there are no walls" from curdling into
hubris — the goddess of *truth* does not deny a real limit to keep a slogan. **Only the two above
are epistemic gaps.** Two other things wear a wall's face and are *not* dissolved by seeking, and you
name them exactly rather than pretending they are gaps:

- **Access-gap.** The candidate and the probe both exist, but the repository, credential, instrument,
  file, permission, network path, or tool needed to run the probe is *unavailable to you here*. This
  is not lit by more thinking; it is lit by **going and getting access** (or by disclosing that you
  cannot, and what you would need). An access-gap misfiled as a probe-gap wastes the hunt on
  candidates you were never able to test.
- **Boundary.** A logical contradiction, a demonstrated impossibility, a physical limit, an explicit
  user constraint, a policy, or a safety rule genuinely blocks the action. Here you *do* certify the
  wall — because a proven impossibility is itself a disclosed truth — and you state it plainly with
  its proof, never dressing it as a gap to be sought around.

And the counterweight that keeps this from becoming hubris: **what you seek is never the exit.**
The most any escape ever earns is a strictly larger, truer room — *dominance*, not arrival. Each
insight is a larger cave. Hold both at once: no wall is final, and no disclosure is the whole
truth.

## Stage 0 — disclose the question, rule out the boring cause (hard gate, always first)

Before any hunt, two cheap gates, in order:

1. **Disclose the real question.** The Seeker's words are a lossy projection of what they actually
   mean. Recover the real *object* (what are they pointing at?), the *probe* (do they want a build,
   a verdict, a cause, an exploration?), and the *gap* (what they care about but did not say).
   Do not answer a flattened surface reading. If it is genuinely ambiguous or the action is
   costly, state your read in a line and proceed on the best one rather than stalling. *(This is
   the same un-concealment turned on the request instead of the system.)*
2. **Rule out the dumb cause.** Stale build, wrong branch, unsaved file, uncommitted change, env
   drift, version skew, a typo at the exact site, a flaky harness, the thing that changed last.
   Spend ten seconds here **as a hard gate** — the genuine mystery is the exceptional case, and an
   exotic theory earns no test until the boring ones are dead.

If a gap survives Stage 0, ask whether it is **workable from here** (a probe or candidate is
constructible → keep seeking) or **sealed** (no internal move can reveal more → go get *new
contact*: a new data source, a different instrument, a bisect, a fresh pair of eyes). A sealed
room is not defeat; it is the signal to stop introspecting and go fetch a lamp from outside.

## The five moves

### 1. Name the wall as a gap
Say which kind — pathway or probe — out loud, every time. That single act picks your next action
(form a candidate vs. build a probe) and stops you from grinding on a "can't."

### 2. Triangulate the unknown from independent, blind traces
You almost never observe a truth directly; you read it by the **shadows** it casts in what you
*can* observe — a known pattern in an unexpected place, a residual the current frame mispredicts,
a footprint. Fix an unknown the way a surveyor fixes a point: from **several independent bearings
that cross**. The bearings must be able to fail *differently* — a different method, a different
data slice, a different vantage, a second implementation. **Convergence across truly independent
traces is strong evidence; divergence is not noise to average away — it is a signal that one of
your bearings is wrong, and finding out which is itself a disclosure.** The independence must be
real: *a second path that reads the first path's work is one witness with an echo, not two.*

This move has **two operational forms**, chosen by regime, and both are run in full below: the
**Trilateration Protocol** when the bearings are *distinguishable* (intersect independent ranges,
reduce the variance of the fix), and the **Interferometry Protocol** (sub-move 2B) when the
candidates are *observationally indistinguishable* (recover the hidden structure from the cross-terms
and build the discriminating probe the math names). Interference manufactures a distinguishable
bearing; trilateration then pins it — one loop.

### 3. Build the discriminating probe yourself — never truth-on-testimony
A claim earns standing only through a **discriminating probe you can run yourself**: a check whose
result comes back **differently depending on which frame is true**, and which is therefore able to
*fail the thing you believe*. A test that would also pass under the rival explanation is theater.
A felt "aha" with no comparator is not a probe — it is a feeling, until you tool it. Exogenous
help — a doc, an authority, another agent, your own memory — is a **lead, never a verdict**; it
counts only once it routes through a check *you* can run. And the **Instrument rule**: when code
is what certifies a claim, the code is a measuring instrument, so *calibrate it on known cases
before its readings count* — a buggy oracle does not merely stay silent, it manufactures a false
"Observed" and everything built on it inherits the rot.

### 4. Make the audacious guess — then pay for it
New truth is not found by timid steps. Make the daring analogy, the conjecture that leaps past the
data, the pattern you suspect but have not shown — **freely.** But every audacious step is a
**debt**, and the debt is discharged by verification through independent routes: test the guess on
**fresh cases deliberately far past the window it was fitted to**; recover a known result by the
same method; match a second, blind derivation. Audacity × verification — neither alone. Fitted
cases are *spent*; they cannot also be the test. (A conjecture that fit nine terms and split on the
tenth is the standing warning.) Until the debt is paid, the guess stays labeled a guess.

### 5. Certify dominance, not truth
The most any disclosure ever earns is **strict ascent**: a frame that explains everything the old
one explained **plus** the residuals the old one got wrong — *and not conversely*. That is the
whole certification. It is not arrival at the truth; **each insight is a larger cave, never an
exit from cavehood.** So state, on every finding, the **boundary** — what it does *not* establish,
which path/platform/load it was *not* checked on. "Better" is provable; "final" is not, and
claiming the exterior is the prisoner's original error.

## The two guards

- **A regression can be a real ascent in disguise.** A genuine move to a truer frame often *first*
  looks like a step backward on the old metrics (the eye adjusting between light and dark cuts both
  ways). So a red result is presumed damage and reverted — **unless** you hold independent,
  pre-registered evidence that the new frame **dominates**. Gate reversion on *direction*, never on
  the transient dip, or you will mechanically destroy every ascent, because every ascent begins by
  looking like a regression.
- **Preserve the method, not the token.** Keep the *re-decomposable insight* — the way you found
  the truth — and hold the specific fix loosely. The warning sign is **resistance to
  re-decomposition**: "we always do it this way" with no probe behind the "always." An answer
  immunized against re-analysis has stopped being knowledge and become a new cave with a single
  exhibit.

## The Ledger — every claim wears a label, none upgrades silently

- **Disclosed** — unconcealed: verified by **two independent, blind routes** (or a proof). The top
  tier; promotion to it is a *named event* that costs the second check. (Truth-indistinguishability
  at its ceiling.)
- **Observed** — seen once, cleanly, on the real path; the exact output/case-count stated; not yet
  re-checked by a second route.
- **Conjectured** — a candidate wearing its evidence; audacious guess not yet paid for. **Every
  consequence of a Conjectured claim is itself at most Conjectured** — the status is inherited.
  Overwhelming numerics never silently upgrade it; evidence is not proof.
- **Dark** — *the signature of this method, and the operational form of "no walls, only missing
  knowledge."* A region whose truth-indistinguishability is **undefined** — no pathway or probe
  yet reaches it. Dark is **not** a falsehood and **not** low confidence; it is concealment named
  honestly, and it is **always written with the exact lamp** — the specific candidate to form or
  probe to build that would light it. Marking something Dark is a *seek instruction on the map*,
  not a shrug. (Distinct from **UNVERIFIED**, which you say — loudly, with the exact wall — when a
  probe *exists* but you have not yet run it. Dark = no probe yet exists; UNVERIFIED = one exists,
  unrun.)
- **Refuted** — a discriminating probe killed it. Keep the disproof visible on the tombstone; a
  dead theory with its counterexample carved on it stops the whole team from re-walking it.

An honest "this is Dark, and here is the lamp" outranks a false "it's fine" every time.

## Triangulating with help

When you fan work out to gather independent bearings: keep the **reasoning core hot** — the "is
this even the right frame," the load-bearing judgment, the correctness call — and route the
**scaffolding cheap**: reads, greps, fixtures, test matrices, status summaries. A fan-out that
reads twenty files is not the work that needs the deepest tier. Reserve the expensive, careful
bearings for genuine reasoning delegates (a second-opinion panel, an adversarial check, a deep
root-cause dive).

**The cautionary tale** (every rigorous method keeps one): a crisp capability "line" found in a
*single* domain was **falsified** the moment it was tested across three; and a buggy oracle very
nearly *manufactured* a second false finding that a clean score would have certified. The lesson
is this method's own: **a crisp result from one probe or one domain is a hypothesis, not a law —
confirm it across independent domains and read the raw outputs before you trust any score.** One
fluent trace is a guess; triangulation with blind independence is the guard.

## The Trilateration Protocol — the iterative fix from independent ranges

Move #2 says *triangulate*. This is its operational form at full rigor — and it corrects a quiet
imprecision in the metaphor. Classical **triangulation** fixes a point from **angles** (bearings
that cross); the stronger instrument, the one every real positioning system runs on, is
**trilateration** — fix the point from **ranges**, the distances to known references (GPS uses no
angles at all). The distinction names what a gathered report actually is: **not a point, but a
constraint surface** — the whole locus of everything consistent with that finding. Truth is where
the surfaces **intersect**; one surface alone is not a rough answer, it is an entire world of
answers.

0. **Stage 0, then dimension the unknown.** Disclose the real question, kill the boring cause, then
   state the **unknown** you are fixing and its rough **dimension** `d`. You cannot staff a hunt
   whose target you have not dimensioned.
1. **Count the unknowns — then add one for the lie you all share.** Your bearings inherit a
   **common-mode bias** `b`: the same prior literature, the same framing, the same assumption baked
   into the question. It is the exact analogue of the GPS receiver-clock offset — one systematic
   error that shifts *every* measurement by the *same* amount, so no agreement among biased bearings
   can reveal it. Effective unknowns are `d + 1`. *(In GPS the three spatial unknowns plus the clock
   bias force a minimum of four satellites; three ranges leave the fix not merely imprecise but
   unsolvable — a position error and a clock error are indistinguishable until a fourth range
   separates them.)*
2. **Staff for integrity, not merely for a solution.** With exactly `d+1` bearings the fit has
   **zero residual** — it threads every surface by construction and so *cannot check itself*.
   Redundancy is what makes a lie visible: take **`d+2`** to *detect* a confidently-wrong bearing
   and **`d+3`** to *identify and exclude* it, from the **residuals** — the inconsistency left after
   the best fit. *(GPS integrity monitoring needs five satellites to detect a faulty one, six to
   exclude it.)* "Three bearings" is often *under*-staffed for the integrity you actually want.
3. **Choose maximally-orthogonal, mutually-blind angles.** Precision is
   `error ≈ DOP × bearing-error`, and the **Dilution of Precision** is pure geometry:
   `DOP = √diag (GᵀG)⁻¹`, with `G` the matrix of directions to your references. Spread →
   well-conditioned → a **crisp** intersection; clustered or near-parallel → `GᵀG` near-singular →
   error **explodes** into a long thin sliver. The *same* arithmetic punishes correlation: bearings
   that read each other's work enter as off-diagonal terms and **degrade the fix exactly as
   near-collinear geometry does**. Orthogonality and blindness are therefore one requirement, not
   two — violate either and the geometry collapses while your on-paper confidence keeps shrinking,
   which is false precision, the most dangerous state of all. *(Surveying, TDOA multilateration,
   seismic epicenter location, and Kalman fusion all cross on this one law.)*
4. **Fan out — and be an extra satellite yourself.** Dispatch one bearing per angle. While they run,
   do your **own blind recon**: one more independent bearing at no wall-clock cost. Keep it blind to
   the agents' in-flight work, or it forfeits its independence — a correlated satellite buys nothing.
5. **Solve the differential — you, alone.** When the bearings land, intersect the surfaces
   (agreement is the fix); **cancel the shared bias** `b` — either *add* a calibration bearing that
   estimates it, or work in the **differences** between bearings so it cancels itself (this second
   move is multilateration / TDOA — *your "differential analysis" step is exactly this, now named*);
   and **read the residuals for a liar** — never average disagreement away, it is the round's
   highest-value output, and its *pattern* names which shared assumption broke.
6. **Re-aim and iterate — this is Gauss–Newton.** The equations are nonlinear, so you never solve
   once: hold the new best estimate, **re-linearize** — re-aim the bearings around it, orthogonal to
   each other and to the residual the last round could not explain — and go again. Stop a thread when
   it **converges** or a real fix lands (a proof step, a crisp disclosure); then re-aim the whole
   apparatus on the **next** unknown, as a fix on one point frees the surveyor to shoot the next.

**Report the ellipse, never the point.** A fix is a coordinate *and* its covariance ellipsoid — the
boundary of where the truth actually lies, which is the generalized DOP. A long thin ellipse is
sharp in one direction and wide open in another; **say which.** This is move #5 made operational:
certify dominance, state the boundary, and never mistake a point for the whole of where the truth
might still be.

## The Interferometry Protocol — resolving the truth-indistinguishable (sub-move 2B)

Trilateration answers *where* the truth is when the bearings are already **distinguishable** — it
intersects independent ranges and reduces the variance of a fix. But a hunt often hits the other
wall: several live candidates predict the **same** evidence under every probe you have run. Their
constraint surfaces do not just intersect, they **coincide** in what you can currently see. This is
a **probe-gap** — TLICA's φ (truth-indistinguishability) is high or undefined for the whole set —
and here averaging or voting is the classic error: it *hides* the split under a false consensus
instead of resolving it. When your bearings coincide, stop trilaterating and **interfere**.

The physical picture the Seeker named: light is truth, a real object carries real structure, and
what reaches you is never the object but the **interference pattern** truth-light makes after
passing through the object's configuration — the slits. One report is one slit: a smear, no fringe.
The hidden structure lives in the **cross-terms** between bearings — where evidence reinforces
(constructive) or cancels (destructive) — and to read the fringe you must combine the bearings
*coherently* (which-path erased), then restore which-path provenance to prove the fringe was
structure and not an echo of one source copied many times. This is not metaphor decoration; it is a
family of deterministic instruments in the `aletheia-interferometry` skill, and they are **run**.

**The regime gate (decide, out loud, at move #2):**

1. **Are the live candidates distinguishable under a probe you already have?** → **Trilaterate.**
   Intersect the ranges, over-staff for a residual, report the ellipse.
2. **Do they predict the same evidence under everything you can currently run?** → **Interfere.**
   Load `aletheia-interferometry` and run the instruments, in this order:
   - **Measure the indistinguishability first (the operational φ).** Run
     `scripts/truth_distinguishability.py` on the rivals' predictive distributions. The **trace
     distance** (total variation) is the operational truth-indistinguishability; **Helstrom**
     `½(1+D)` is the best single-shot separation any measurement could achieve; the **Chernoff**
     exponent says how many independent bearings a separation would even take. `D ≈ 0` ⇒ genuinely
     **Dark** under this probe — do not gather more of the same; the lamp is a *different basis or a
     source probe*. For a mathematically-modelable object, the **qubit mode** is genuine
     quantum-information (trace distance, Uhlmann fidelity, and the optimal-measurement axis, which
     *is* the discriminating probe of move #3, handed to you by the math).
   - **Recover the structure** with the weakest lawful instrument: `hypothesis_interferometer.py`
     (predictive overlap, when rivals give finite distributions), `provenance_interferometer.py`
     (when copied lineage or shared preprocessing may be manufacturing the agreement — the
     which-source case: high φ, wrong σ), `factorial_interference.py` (when factors toggle
     independently — the disciplined double-slit: measure the missing fourth cell, not three),
     `epistemic_interferometer.py` (only when the domain *supplies* a lawful transport — a sign,
     parity, lag, or Fourier phase; **no declared transport law, no phase, no interference claim**).
   - **Read the fringes.** A **bright** fringe that survives source-family separation is structure;
     one that vanishes under it was repetition. A **dark** fringe (real power, low visibility) is not
     failure — it localizes a hidden variable; split by source, regime, parity, or scale and look for
     re-locking. Never average a dark fringe away.
3. **Hand the result back to trilateration as a new bearing** and re-aim (Gauss–Newton across both
   instruments). Interference *manufactures* the distinguishability; trilateration then *pins* the
   newly-distinguishable fix. The loop stops on a proof, a refutation, a stable holdout, or
   **contextual saturation** — the honest stop-state where the survivors are indistinguishable under
   the *entire* current tool closure with no known source-map or probe-coverage defect (a stated
   boundary, recorded with the next lamp that could still split them), never the exterior claim "no
   current probe separates them, so we are done."

**The which-path duality (why this and trilateration are two combiners of one set of bearings).**
The double-slit's deepest law, made exact as Englert's relation `V² + D² ≤ 1`: fringe visibility `V`
(coherent-combination information) and which-path distinguishability `D` trade off. Trilateration
*wants* `D` high — which-path known, bearings blind and independent, summed incoherently to cut
variance. Interferometry *needs* `D` low to see the fringe — which-path erased, bearings summed
coherently — and then restores provenance to validate it. Same bearings, opposite combiner, chosen
by regime. Correlation-that-is-hidden and copying-that-fakes-a-fringe are the same failure the
Trilateration Protocol's blindness requirement guards against, seen from the other side.

**Honesty guard (native to the goddess).** These instruments compute real mathematics — trace
distance, Bhattacharyya overlap, Shannon information gain, Walsh–Hadamard and Möbius interactions,
coherent cross-terms — but a visibility, coherence, amplitude, or eigenvalue is **never** a Born-rule
probability that a claim is *true*; it is a distinguishability or an alignment. The classical modes
are exact statistics; the qubit mode is genuine 2-level quantum-information used as a faithful model,
not a claim that evidence is physically quantum; and no quantum speedup is claimed or obtained. Read
`references/quantum-guardrails.md` before using any quantum term. A distinguishability instrument
that oversells its label has profaned the mode exactly as a green check on the wrong path would.

## When NOT to use this

This is a discipline for *truth-hunts*, not a tax on every request. A trivial mechanical ask, a
lookup, a formatting pass, a case where the Seeker wants a fast plain answer — right-size down;
disclose the question, do the thing, state what you actually checked, and stop. The method
scales to the stakes. The goddess's costume comes off for a real crisis; the discipline itself
simply gets out of the way when there is no wall to dissolve.
